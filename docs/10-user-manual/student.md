# Student User Manual (`student.md`)

**Document Version:** 1.0.0  
**Target Role:** Enrolled Learner (`STUDENT`)  
**Default Demo Login:** `student@lms.edu` / `Student@123`  

---

## 1. Student Learning Dashboard
After logging in at `/login`, your **Student Dashboard** (`/student/dashboard`) displays your real-time academic and financial standing:
- **Weighted Course Completion Gauge:** Shows your overall completion % (`50% Lectures + 30% Assignments + 20% Quizzes`).
- **Attendance Health Card:** Displays your cumulative attendance % (`>= 75%` required for certificate eligibility).
- **Fee & Eligibility Status:** Shows your paid and pending fee balance plus upcoming assignment and quiz deadlines.

---

## 2. Watching Lectures & Downloading Study Materials
1. Navigate to **My Classroom** (`/student/courses`) and click **Continue Learning** on your enrolled course.
2. Select any lecture from the module sidebar to watch the video lesson and read lecture notes.
3. Your playback progress is saved automatically. When you finish watching a lecture (or click **Mark as Completed**), your course completion percentage updates immediately.
4. Open the **Attached Materials** tab below the player to download PDF notes, slides, and practice files.

---

## 3. Submitting Assignments
1. Open **Assignments** (`/student/assignments`) to view pending and graded coursework.
2. Click **Submit Assignment** on an active assignment, attach your solution file (`.zip`, `.pdf`, `.docx`, `.py`, etc., up to 25 MB) or enter your submission text/link, and click **Upload Submission**.
   - **Tip:** Submit before the `Due Date` to avoid late submission penalties!
3. Once your instructor grades your work, return to this page to view your **Marks Obtained** and **Instructor Rubric Feedback**.

---

## 4. Taking Timed Quizzes (Anti-Cheat Rules)
1. Navigate to **Quizzes** (`/student/quizzes`) and click **Start Timed Quiz**.
2. Once started, the **Authoritative Server Timer** counts down at the top of the screen.
3. **Important Anti-Cheat Guidelines:**
   - Keep the quiz window focused and in fullscreen mode throughout your attempt.
   - Switching browser tabs, minimizing the window, or attempting restricted clipboard actions logs an anti-cheat warning (`Warning X of 3`).
   - Exceeding the maximum warning limit automatically terminates and submits your quiz attempt.
4. Click **Submit Final Quiz** before the timer expires to view your instant auto-graded score and pass/fail result.

---

## 5. Viewing Attendance, Fee Ledger & Receipts
- **My Attendance (`/student/attendance`):** View your session-by-session attendance calendar (`PRESENT`, `LATE`, `ABSENT`, `EXCUSED`, `HOLIDAY`) and see exactly how your attendance percentage is calculated.
- **Fee & Receipts (`/student/payments`):** View your total fee, scholarship discount, paid installments, pending balance, and download official **Payment Receipt Vouchers**.

---

## 6. Downloading & Sharing Your Certificate
1. Once you achieve **>= 80% Course Completion**, **>= 75% Attendance**, passing assessment scores, and **$0.00 Pending Fee**, your certificate becomes available under **My Certificates** (`/student/certificates`).
2. Click **Download Certificate PDF** or copy your unique **Public Verification Link** (`/verify/CERT-...`) to share with employers on LinkedIn or resumes.
